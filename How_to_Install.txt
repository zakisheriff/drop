Drop - Installation Instructions

1. Drag and drop the "Drop" app icon into the "Applications" shortcut folder next to it.
2. Open the app from your Applications folder. When the system warning alert displays, close the dialog.
3. Open System Settings on your Mac.
4. Click "Privacy & Security" in the left sidebar.
5. Scroll down to the "Security" section.
6. You will see a message: "'Drop' was blocked to protect your Mac."
7. Click the "Open Anyway" button next to it.
8. Enter your Mac password/Touch ID to confirm, then click "Open".

Drop will now run perfectly! You only need to perform these steps once.
