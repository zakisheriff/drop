Drop - Installation Instructions

Because Drop is a native utility distributed directly outside the Mac App Store, macOS Gatekeeper might show a prompt when you first open the app (e.g., warning that it's from an unidentified developer). This is normal for unnotarized independent developer builds.

To launch the app:
1. Open Drop.app. When the system warning alert displays, close the dialog.
2. Open System Settings on your Mac.
3. Click "Privacy & Security" in the left sidebar.
4. Scroll down to the "Security" section.
5. You will see a message: "'Drop' was blocked to protect your Mac."
6. Click the "Open Anyway" button next to it.
7. Enter your Mac password/Touch ID to confirm, then click "Open".

Drop will now open and run perfectly! You only need to perform these steps once.
